SATORUS - MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Version 1.0 – effective 20 July 2026 This is the version currently in force. Download PDF

This Master Services Agreement forms part of the Agreement between Satorus Group Limited and the Customer. It is incorporated into the Agreement by, and takes effect on the Effective Date set out in, an Order Form signed by the parties. The Customer agrees to this Master Services Agreement by signing the Order Form, and no separate signature of this document is required. The version of this Master Services Agreement in force on the Effective Date applies for the Term, unless varied in accordance with its terms.
