1. ABOUT SATORUS

Satorus Group Limited, incorporated and registered in England and Wales with company number 16479677 whose registered office is at 59 Whitelands Crescent, London, England, SW18 5QY (“Satorus”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates a platform called “Sidney” which provides intelligence services (“Services”) on behalf of other organisations and businesses (“Business Customers”).

Satorus is the data controller of the personal data of end-users that our Business Customers authorise to use Sidney and this Privacy Notice sets out how we collect and use information during your use of Sidney including:

1.1 individuals who visit https://satorusgroup.com/ (“Website”)

1.2 individual end-users who are authorised by our Customers to use Sidney; and

1.3 individuals who contact us with any queries.

collectively (“You”)

For individuals whose personal data is processed by our Business Customers during their use of Sidney, we are not the data controller of your personal data and we only act as a data processor on behalf of our Business Customers in providing the Services. Please contact the relevant Business Customer who uses Sidney as they are the data controller of your personal data.

Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns at: beth@satorusgroup.com

2. HOW DO WE COLLECT PERSONAL DATA?

We collect personal data from a variety of sources such as:

  • from you directly when you contact us with a query;
  • from information we generate about you during our relationship with you such as: technical data collected from cookies and other similar technologies which are described in our Cookies Policy when you use our Website and/or Sidney; and
  • from information we collect about you from third parties such as: the Business Customer who engages us to provide the Services and our service providers who assist us in providing our Services. This may include publicly available sources too, such as social media.

3. WHAT PERSONAL DATA WILL WE COLLECT AND WHY?

We may process the following information about you in connection with our business activities.

3.1 Business contact information: such as your name, role, and contact details such as your work email address and work telephone number.

3.2 User log-in information: such as your work email address.

3.3 Marketing information: such as when we promote our Services via direct marketing communications such as email.

3.4 Technical information collected from your device when you are browsing on our Website, or our digital communications such as emails or using Sidney. This includes: your IP address, your location (by country, state and city), device type, browser type, operating system, interaction with, and responses to, our marketing communication and activity on our Website and Sidney. Please see our cookies policy for more information.

3.5 Correspondence between you and us such as: by email, Website and social media.

3.6 Other: any other information you voluntarily provide.

5. AUTOMATED DECISION MAKING

As part of our Services to the Business Customer, we use artificial intelligence and large language models via Sidney, to assist Business Customers. The output of Sidney is advisory only. No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made by us in this respect given the Business Customer always makes the final decision which includes meaningful human intervention.

6. SHARING YOUR PERSONAL DATA

Sometimes we use third parties who are contractually engaged to provide services to us that may involve the sharing of personal data in order to provide such services. For example:

  • Companies that help us: such as service providers who assist us with user authentication, cloud hosting and storage, mapping services and AI model providers. This is on the basis of our legitimate interests of ours and of such third party service providers, to carry out the services we have requested.
  • Our professional advisers: including IT services, legal and accountancy services that assist us in carrying out our business activities in our legitimate interests.
  • Other companies we work closely with: for security, assistance, improving our Services, and reporting, based on our legitimate business interests.
  • In the context of a transaction: we may share your personal data with potential partners and other third parties in connection with the consideration, negotiation, or completion of a corporate transaction in which we are acquired by or merged with another company or we sell or transfer all or a portion of our assets or business. This is on the basis of our legitimate interests for our business operations and the legitimate interests of third parties such as those in connection with the transaction.
  • Government authorities, courts, law enforcement, fraud prevention agencies, the police or other regulatory bodies as required by law in connection with the investigation of crimes. This is on the basis of our legitimate interests for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims to protect our business or in compliance with applicable legal obligations where we believe it is necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation, or otherwise to protect our rights or the rights of a third party.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Although we are based in the UK, our business is global and so we may need to transfer your personal information outside the UK.

Wherever your information is transferred to organisations outside the UK, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect personal information as required by data protection laws. Such safeguards include: standard data protection clauses (including the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Agreement), legal exceptions, adequacy decisions, recognised frameworks (e.g. the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the UK extension to the Framework). If you are located in the UK or the EEA, you may contact us for a copy of the safeguards.

8. HOW WE RETAIN YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We will only keep your personal information for as long as we need to in line with our retention criteria taking into consideration: legal obligations, best industry practice, limitation periods, our business needs and for the establishment, exercise or defence of a legal claims.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

You have a number of rights in respect of your personal data, including the right to request:

  • access to the personal data that we hold about you;
  • an objection to the processing of personal data and that we do not process your personal data for direct marketing purposes;
  • that inaccurate personal data we hold about you is corrected;
  • that your personal data is deleted;
  • that your personal data is provided to you in a portable format.

Please note that most of these rights are not absolute and are limited to certain defined circumstances.

If the basis of our processing of your personal information is ‘consent’ you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness prior to the withdrawal of your consent. However, note that your data may also be being processed subject to another lawful basis as set out above.

10. COMPLAINTS

We encourage you to raise your concerns directly with us so that we can address them through our internal complaints escalation procedure. To do so, please contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding your personal data, at beth@satorusgroup.com

In the UK, if you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate the matter via our complaints procedure by contacting us. During this process we will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, make enquiries into the complaint without undue delay and make an appropriate level of enquiries.

If after the complaints process, you remain dissatisfied, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): www.ico.org.uk

We may update or amend this Privacy Notice from time to time by posting this Notice on our Website, on Sidney and at the footer of our email communications.
