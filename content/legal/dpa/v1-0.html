2. BACKGROUND

2.1 The Customer and Satorus have entered into an Agreement, formed by the Customer’s and Satorus’ execution of an order form (the “Order Form”) which incorporates by reference the Satorus Master Services Agreement (“Master Services Agreement”) and this DPA, and which may require Satorus to process Personal Data on behalf of the Customer. This DPA is agreed by the Customer, and is deemed executed by both parties, upon signature of the Order Form; no separate signature of this DPA is required. This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date set out in the Order Form, and the version of this DPA in force on the Effective Date applies for the Term unless varied in accordance with the Agreement.

2.2 This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) sets out the additional terms, requirements and conditions on which the parties will process and share Personal Data during the Services under the Master Services Agreement. This DPA contains the mandatory clauses required by Article 28(3) of the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the General Data Protection Regulation ((EU) 2016/679) for contracts between controllers and processors.

3. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this DPA, the following definitions shall apply:

Applicable Laws: means:

a) To the extent the UK GDPR applies, the law of the United Kingdom or of a part of the United Kingdom.

b) To the extent the EU GDPR applies, the law of the European Union or any member state of the European Union to which Satorus is subject.

Applicable Data Protection Laws: means:

a) To the extent the UK GDPR applies, the law of the United Kingdom or of a part of the United Kingdom which relates to the protection of Personal Data.

b) To the extent the EU GDPR applies, the law of the European Union or any member state of the European Union to which Satorus is subject, which relates to the protection of Personal Data.

Business Day: a day, other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England, when banks in London are open for business.

Business Hours: the period from 9.00 am to 5.00 pm on a Business Day.

Services: means the services provided by Satorus via the Sidney platform (“Platform”) under the Master Services Agreement.

Shared Personal Data: any Personal Data which the Customer transfers to Satorus; any Personal Data which Satorus Processes in connection with the Master Services Agreement; and as set out in ANNEX A.

Term: this DPA's term as defined in Clause 10.

EU GDPR: the General Data Protection Regulation ((EU) 2016/679).

UK GDPR: has the meaning given to it in section 3(10) (as supplemented by section 205(4)) of the Data Protection Act 2018.

For the purposes of this DPA, the terms, Controller, Data Subject, Personal Data, Personal Data Breach, Processor and Processing, shall have the meaning given to them in Applicable Data Protection Laws, Commissioner shall have the meaning given to it in the UK GDPR and Supervisory Authority shall have the meaning given to it in the EU GDPR.

5. PROCESSING TERMS

5.1 In relation to the Shared Personal Data, ANNEX A sets out the scope, nature and purpose of Processing by Satorus, the duration of the Processing and the types of Shared Personal Data and categories of Data Subject.

5.2 Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph 4.1 Satorus shall, in relation to Shared Personal Data Processed for the purposes of Services:

(a) process that Shared Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Customer, set out in ANNEX A, unless Satorus is required by Applicable Laws to otherwise process that Shared Personal Data. Where Satorus is relying on Applicable Laws as the basis for processing Shared Personal Data, Satorus shall promptly notify the Customer of this unless those Applicable Laws prohibit Satorus from so notifying the Customer on important grounds of public interest. Satorus shall immediately inform the Customer if, in the opinion of Satorus, the instructions of the Customer infringe Applicable Data Protection Laws;

(b) implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect against unauthorised or unlawful processing of Shared Personal Data and against its accidental loss, damage or destruction, including inter alia as appropriate:

(i) the pseudonymisation and encryption of Shared Personal Data;

(ii) the ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of processing systems and Services;

(iii) the ability to restore the availability and access to Shared Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident; and

(iv) a process for regularly testing, assessing and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organisational measures for ensuring the security of the processing.

In assessing the appropriate level of security Satorus shall take into account in particular of the risks that are presented by the processing, in particular from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Shared Personal Data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed.

(c) ensure, and procure, that any personnel engaged and authorised by Satorus to process Shared Personal Data are subject to appropriate obligations of confidentiality;

(d) subject to clause 5.3(f), where the Customer is unable to use the Platform, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Satorus, to assist the Customer, at the Customer’s own expense, in responding to requests from a Data Subject under Applicable Data Protection Laws and in ensuring compliance with the Customer's obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws with respect to security, Personal Data Breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and consultations with the Commissioner, Supervisory Authorities or other regulators and, in particular, Satorus shall promptly notify the Customer if it receives any complaint, notice or communication (whether from the Commissioner, any Data Subject, Supervisory Authority or other third party) which relates to processing of Shared Personal Data;

(e) notify the Customer without undue delay on becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach;

(f) at the written direction of the Customer, delete or return to the Customer all Shared Personal Data on termination or expiry of this DPA, and certify to the Customer in writing it has done so, unless Satorus is required by Applicable Law to continue to process that Shared Personal Data, in which case Satorus shall promptly notify the Customer, in writing, of what that Applicable Law is and shall only be permitted to process that Shared Personal Data for the specific purpose so-notified, and all other requirements set out in this DPA shall continue to apply to such Shared Personal Data notwithstanding the termination or expiry of this DPA for as long as such Shared Personal Data is processed by Satorus; and

(g) on the Customer's request, makes available such information to demonstrate compliance with the obligations in this DPA as the Customer may reasonably request with 3 months’ written notice, and allow for one audit per calendar year at the Customer’s own costs, including inspections, by the Customer or the Customer's designated auditor, to demonstrate its compliance with Applicable Data Protection Laws and this paragraph 5. The Customer and the Customer’s designated auditor shall enter into a confidentiality agreement prior to the audit commencing and shall make all of the relevant audit reports available to Satorus for review.

5.3 In relation to the Shared Personal Data, the Customer shall:

(a) comply with all obligations and requirements to which they are subject as a Controller under the Applicable Data Protection Laws;

(b) ensure it has a lawful basis under the Applicable Data Protection Laws for the use of the Services and the collecting, Processing and sharing of Shared Personal Data with Satorus for the purposes of the Services and as set out in this DPA;

(c) ensure it has carried all required assessments including but not limited to data protection impact assessments, legitimate interests assessments and appropriate policy documents as required under Applicable Data Protection Laws for the use of the Services and the collecting, Processing and sharing of Shared Personal Data with Satorus for the purposes of the Services and as set out in this DPA;

(d) where the Shared Personal Data includes special categories of personal data or personal data relating to criminal convictions and offences, ensure that a condition for Processing under Article 9 and/or Article 10 of the UK GDPR (or the equivalent provisions of the EU GDPR) and, where required, Schedule 1 to the Data Protection Act 2018 is satisfied in respect of that Processing, and maintain any appropriate policy document required in connection with that condition;

(e) in respect of any Personal Data which the Customer itself provides or transfers to Satorus, ensure it has all necessary and appropriate valid consents and notices, or another valid lawful basis, under Applicable Data Protection Laws to enable the lawful transfer of such Personal Data to Satorus for the duration of and purposes of the Services and as set out in this DPA;

(f) provide clear and sufficient information to Data Subjects, in accordance with the Applicable Data Protection Laws, including but not limited to, the purposes for which it will process their Personal Data, the legal basis for such purposes, the sharing of Shared Personal Data with recipients and such other information as is required by Applicable Data Protection Laws, save to the extent that an exemption from such transparency obligations applies under Applicable Data Protection Laws (including where the provision of such information to the Data Subject proves impossible or would involve a disproportionate effort, or would be likely to render impossible or seriously impair the achievement of the objectives of the Processing), in which case the Customer shall document its reliance on that exemption and take appropriate measures to protect the Data Subject’s rights, freedoms and legitimate interests;

(g) that taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Satorus, the Customer shall use the Platform to assist itself in relation to responding to requests from a Data Subject under Applicable Data Protection Laws and in ensuring compliance with the Customer's obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws with respect to security, Personal Data Breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and consultations with the Commissioner, Supervisory Authorities or other regulators;

(h) ensure that meaningful human review and intervention is applied to any decisions or actions taken based on the outputs of the Platform;

(i) maintain policies and procedures to ensure that decisions involving legal, financial, reputational, or other significant impacts on individuals or organisations are subject to meaningful human oversight and review;

(j) provide such assistance as is reasonably required to enable Satorus to comply with Applicable Data Protection Laws in relation to any Shared Personal Data within the time limits imposed by the Data Protection Laws;

(k) where the Customer provides Shared Personal Data to Satorus directly, only do so by using secure methods as agreed between the Parties;

(l) have in place throughout the Term, appropriate technical and organisational security measures to prevent unauthorised or unlawful Processing of the Shared Personal Data or the accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to Shared Personal Data;

(m) comply with its obligations to report a Personal Data Breach to the Information Commissioner or any Supervisory Authority and/or Data Subjects under Applicable Data Protection Laws and shall inform the Satorus of any Personal Data Breach;

(n) provide reasonable assistance as is requested by Satorus to facilitate the handling of any Personal Data Breach in an expeditious and compliant manner; and

(o) ensure that any Shared Personal Data which the Customer itself provides or transfers to Satorus is accurate at the time of transfer.
